fix coulomb/background command

Syntax: 

fix ID coulomb/background Nevery source A_bg Z_bg bg-sources
  • ID is documented in fix command
  • coulomb/background = style name of this fix command
  • Nevery = apply the collision operator every Nevery timesteps
  • source = background fix-ID or plasma TeSRC NeSRC 
      background fix-ID = ID of a fix background
    supplying Te, ne, Ti, ni, parallel flow and B at the particle
  plasma TeSRC NeSRC = per-grid or per-particle sources for the
    electron temperature (eV) and electron density (m^-3)
    used in the Coulomb logarithm
  • A_bg = mass of the background ion species (amu)
  • Z_bg = charge of the background species (units of e, negative for electrons)
  • bg-sources = TiSRC NiSRC VparSRC BxSRC BySRC BzSRC 
      required only with plasma; omitted with background
  TiSRC, NiSRC = background temperature (eV) and density (m^-3)
  VparSRC = background parallel flow speed (m/s)
  BxSRC, BySRC, BzSRC = magnetic field components in OpenEdge
    coordinate order (T)
  • every SRC token is c_ID[col] (column col of a per-grid compute) or p_name (per-particle custom floating-point vector)

Examples: 

fix pd background file plasma.h5 static yes
fix fcb coulomb/background 5 background pd 2.0 1.0 

fix fcb coulomb/background 1 plasma c_cpl[7] c_cpl[10] 2.0 1.0 &
    c_cpl[8] c_cpl[10] c_cpl[11] c_cpl[1] c_cpl[2] c_cpl[3]

Description:

Apply Coulomb collisions between every charged simulation particle and a fluid background plasma. The background is not represented by particles: at each application every charged particle in a cell collides once with a virtual partner of mass A_bg and charge Z_bg whose velocity is drawn from a Maxwellian at the local background temperature Ti and density ni, drifting with the local parallel flow Vpar along the magnetic field direction. Only the simulation particle velocity is changed; the background acts as an infinite reservoir.

The scattering angle follows the Nanbu (1997) cumulative small-angle scheme, in the same form as the Takizuka-Abe pairing operator. For a test particle of charge q and mass m colliding with a partner of charge q_bg and mass m_bg at relative speed g, the scattering parameter is 

s = (q q_bg)^2 n_bg lnLambda dt / (4 pi eps0^2 mu^2 g^3)

where mu is the reduced mass and dt = Nevery * timestep. For s > 6 the scattering is isotropic, for 0.01 < s < 6 the Nanbu A(s) table is used, and for smaller s the small-angle limit cos(chi) = 1 + s ln(U) is sampled. The Coulomb logarithm is evaluated from the local electron temperature and density as 

lnLambda = max(2, ln(12 pi ne lambda_D^3))

with the Debye length lambda_D computed from Te and ne.

With the background form all plasma quantities (Te, ne, Ti, ni, Vpar, B) are sampled at the particle position from the referenced fix background. With the plasma form the Coulomb-logarithm inputs Te and ne and the six background quantities are read from the listed sources, which may be per-grid compute columns (c_ID[col], evaluated at the particle's cell) or per-particle custom vectors (p_name). The fix invokes the referenced computes itself on every step it acts.

Neutral species are not affected. Cells or particles where the background density or temperature is zero are skipped. Particles integrated in guiding-center mode are scattered on their reconstructed physical velocity, after which the guiding-center state (v_par, mu) is re-projected.

The operator is applied at the end of the step, after the particle move.

Restrictions:

This command is part of the OPENEDGE package.

Requires either a fix background or per-grid computes providing the plasma quantities. Only charged species are scattered. The background density that enters s is the fluid density ni from the source; the A_bg and Z_bg values must describe the same species.

Related commands:

fix coulomb/binary, fix background, fix neutral/collide/background

Default: none
