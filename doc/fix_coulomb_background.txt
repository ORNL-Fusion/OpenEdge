fix coulomb/background command :h3

[Syntax:]

fix ID coulomb/background Nevery source A_bg Z_bg bg-sources :pre

ID is documented in "fix"_fix.html command :ulb,l
coulomb/background = style name of this fix command :l
Nevery = apply the collision operator every Nevery timesteps :l
source = {background} fix-ID or {plasma} TeSRC NeSRC :l
  {background} fix-ID = ID of a "fix background"_fix_background.html
    supplying Te, ne, Ti, ni, parallel flow and B at the particle
  {plasma} TeSRC NeSRC = per-grid or per-particle sources for the
    electron temperature (eV) and electron density (m^-3)
    used in the Coulomb logarithm :pre
A_bg = mass of the background ion species (amu) :l
Z_bg = charge of the background species (units of e, negative for electrons) :l
bg-sources = TiSRC NiSRC VparSRC BxSRC BySRC BzSRC :l
  required only with {plasma}; omitted with {background}
  TiSRC, NiSRC = background temperature (eV) and density (m^-3)
  VparSRC = background parallel flow speed (m/s)
  BxSRC, BySRC, BzSRC = magnetic field components in OpenEdge
    coordinate order (T) :pre
every SRC token is {c_ID\[col\]} (column col of a per-grid compute) or {p_name} (per-particle custom floating-point vector) :l
:ule

[Examples:]

fix pd background file plasma.h5 static yes
fix fcb coulomb/background 5 background pd 2.0 1.0 :pre

fix fcb coulomb/background 1 plasma c_cpl\[7\] c_cpl\[10\] 2.0 1.0 &
    c_cpl\[8\] c_cpl\[10\] c_cpl\[11\] c_cpl\[1\] c_cpl\[2\] c_cpl\[3\] :pre

[Description:]

Apply Coulomb collisions between every charged simulation particle and
a fluid background plasma. The background is not represented by
particles: at each application every charged particle in a cell
collides once with a virtual partner of mass {A_bg} and charge {Z_bg}
whose velocity is drawn from a Maxwellian at the local background
temperature Ti and density ni, drifting with the local parallel flow
Vpar along the magnetic field direction. Only the simulation particle
velocity is changed; the background acts as an infinite reservoir.

The scattering angle follows the Nanbu (1997) cumulative
small-angle scheme, in the same form as the Takizuka-Abe pairing
operator. For a test particle of charge q and mass m colliding with a
partner of charge q_bg and mass m_bg at relative speed g, the
scattering parameter is

s = (q q_bg)^2 n_bg lnLambda dt / (4 pi eps0^2 mu^2 g^3) :pre

where mu is the reduced mass and dt = Nevery * timestep. For s > 6 the
scattering is isotropic, for 0.01 < s < 6 the Nanbu A(s) table is
used, and for smaller s the small-angle limit cos(chi) = 1 + s ln(U)
is sampled. The Coulomb logarithm is evaluated from the local
electron temperature and density as

lnLambda = max(2, ln(12 pi ne lambda_D^3)) :pre

with the Debye length lambda_D computed from Te and ne.

With the {background} form all plasma quantities (Te, ne, Ti, ni,
Vpar, B) are sampled at the particle position from the referenced
"fix background"_fix_background.html. With the {plasma} form the
Coulomb-logarithm inputs Te and ne and the six background quantities
are read from the listed sources, which may be per-grid compute
columns ({c_ID\[col\]}, evaluated at the particle's cell) or
per-particle custom vectors ({p_name}). The fix invokes the referenced
computes itself on every step it acts.

Neutral species are not affected. Cells or particles where the
background density or temperature is zero are skipped. Particles
integrated in guiding-center mode are scattered on their reconstructed
physical velocity, after which the guiding-center state (v_par, mu) is
re-projected.

The operator is applied at the end of the step, after the particle
move.

[Restrictions:]

This command is part of the OPENEDGE package.

Requires either a "fix background"_fix_background.html or per-grid
computes providing the plasma quantities. Only charged species are
scattered. The background density that enters {s} is the fluid
density ni from the source; the {A_bg} and {Z_bg} values must describe
the same species.
